Privacy Policy for TalkMate

Last updated: 12 November 2026

TalkMate: Practice English (“TalkMate”, “the App”, “we”, “us”) is operated by Moonlit Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account and using TalkMate, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and profile information you give us, such as your email address, name and the profile details you choose to add. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • Content you share, such as messages, voice room chat and the ratings you give after a conversation.
  • Voice and speech when you use AI features such as Correction summary, speaking games and Interview Prep, so we can give you feedback. Correction summary is optional and off by default, and only your own voice is used, never the other person's.
  • Activity information, such as the calls you take part in, talk time, points, game results and whether you are online.
  • Safety information, such as reports, blocks and actions taken on accounts that break our rules.
  • Device information, such as a device identifier, used to send you notifications and to prevent misuse.

We do not use advertising or analytics tools, and we do not collect your location or contacts.

2. Calls and voice rooms

Calls and voice rooms happen in real time and are encrypted in transit. We do not record or store the audio of your calls or voice rooms.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To match you with practice partners and enable calls, voice rooms and messaging.
  • To give you AI feedback on your speaking, score speaking games and run Interview Prep.
  • To show your profile, online status, ratings and leaderboard position to other members.
  • To award points and run the weekly leaderboard.
  • To send you notifications about calls, messages and people waiting to talk.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove harmful content and suspend accounts, or block phones, that break our rules.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the App.

4. Information shared with other members

Other members can see your profile (including your previous names, ratings and stats), your leaderboard position and the messages you send them. People in a voice room can hear you and see your messages in the room chat. Please don’t share information you wish to keep private.

5. Third-party services

We use trusted third-party service providers to operate, maintain and improve TalkMate. These providers may process certain user information, including account information, audio and text, where necessary to deliver the App’s features.

We require service providers to protect user information and to process it in accordance with applicable data protection requirements and our agreements with them. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing.

6. Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and content while your account is active. Voice audio and transcripts used for AI features are not kept on our servers after you get your result, and messages are removed from our server once they are delivered. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below; we will delete your account data within 30 days of a verified request, except where we must retain certain information to comply with law or to keep the community safe (for example, records of serious rule violations).

7. Security

Connections to our servers and call audio are encrypted in transit using industry-standard methods, and passwords are stored only in a securely scrambled form. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

TalkMate is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old to use the App. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it. See our Child Safety Standards Policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
