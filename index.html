Privacy Policy for TalkMate

Last updated: 22 October 2026

TalkMate: Practice English (“TalkMate”, “the App”, “we”, “us”) is operated by Moonlit Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account and using TalkMate, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile

  • Account information: your email address and a password, or, if you sign in with Google, the email address and name from your Google account. Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are stored only as a salted one-way hash (bcrypt).
  • Profile information you provide: name, username, gender, date of birth, country, native language, short bio and avatar colour.
  • Name history: when you change your name, we keep a record of your previous names. Other members can see this list on your profile.

Conversations

  • Messages: text messages you send are delivered through our server. A message waiting for an offline recipient is held on our server only until it is delivered, then removed from the server. Your conversation history is stored on your own device.
  • Calls: we record that a call took place, who took part and how long you talked (used for ratings, points and the weekly leaderboard). We do not record or store the audio of your calls.
  • Voice rooms: audio in group voice rooms is sent in real time and is not recorded by us. Text messages in a room's chat are shown to the people in that room.
  • Ratings: the like/dislike and star ratings you give or receive after a conversation.

Your voice and speech in AI features

  • Correction summary (optional, off by default): if you turn it on in your profile, your own microphone audio during a call (never the other person's) is streamed to our speech-to-text provider to create a transcript. When the call ends, the transcript is sent to our AI provider to find mistakes, and the corrections are shown to you. We do not store this audio or transcript on our servers.
  • Speaking games and Interview Prep: what you say is recognised on your phone. On some Android versions, a short recording of your answer is also uploaded so it can be transcribed accurately. The text of your answer is sent to our AI provider to score it, give feedback or create the next interview question. Recordings are processed only to give you a result and are not stored on our servers. An Interview Prep session is kept in our server's memory only while it is in progress. Meteor Rush uses only your phone's speech recognition.
  • Read-aloud: Interview Prep questions are read aloud by your phone's built-in text-to-speech.

Points, safety and app use

  • Points and activity: daily check-ins, points you earn or spend, game results, talk time and your weekly leaderboard score.
  • Safety records: reports you make or receive, people you block, and any warnings or account suspensions.
  • Presence: whether you are currently online, used for matchmaking, the Online now list and buddy lists.
  • Notification token: a push notification token for your device, used to send call, message and Speak-now notifications.

We do not use advertising or analytics tools, and we do not collect your location or contacts.

2. How your calls work (audio)

Voice calls use WebRTC and are sent in real time between participants. Audio is encrypted in transit (DTLS-SRTP). When a direct connection isn’t possible, audio is relayed through a TURN server purely to pass it along. It is not recorded or retained. We do not store call audio anywhere. If you turn on Correction summary, only your own voice is transcribed as described above.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To match you with practice partners and enable calls, voice rooms and messaging.
  • To give you AI feedback on your speaking, score speaking games and run Interview Prep.
  • To show your profile, online status, ratings and leaderboard position to other members.
  • To award points and run the weekly leaderboard.
  • To send you notifications about calls, messages and people waiting to talk.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove harmful content and suspend accounts that break our rules.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the App.

4. Information shared with other members

By design, other TalkMate members can see your profile (name, username, avatar, bio, country, language, previous names, and your ratings and stats), your position on the weekly leaderboard, and the messages you send them. People in a voice room can hear you when you speak and see your messages in the room chat. Please don’t share information you wish to keep private.

5. Service providers

We use these third-party services to run the App. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Render (server hosting) and Neon (database).
  • Deepgram (speech-to-text) for Correction summary, speaking games and Interview Prep.
  • Groq (AI language model) for corrections, game scoring, Interview Prep and voice room quizzes. If it is unavailable, Google (Gemini) or OpenAI may be used as a backup for corrections.
  • Agora to carry audio in group voice rooms.
  • Metered (TURN relay) to relay call audio when a direct connection cannot be made.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications, and Google Sign-In if you choose to sign in with Google.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing.

6. Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and content while your account is active. Voice audio and transcripts used for AI features are not kept on our servers after you get your result, and messages are removed from our server once they are delivered. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below; we will delete your account data within 30 days of a verified request, except where we must retain certain information to comply with law or to keep the community safe (for example, records of serious rule violations).

7. Security

Connections to our servers use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), call audio uses DTLS-SRTP, and passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

TalkMate is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old to use the App. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it. See our Child Safety Standards Policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
